# IMBOPwiki — Product Definition

## 1. Purpose
IMBOPwiki is the shared knowledge, collaboration and communication layer of IMBOP.

It gives institutional economic networks a governed place to discover, discuss, create, preserve and reuse knowledge around markets, opportunities, research, reports, trends, value chains and member collaboration.

It is an in-app capability inside IMBOP, not an external documentation site.

## 2. Product Thesis
IMBOP connects:

**Institution → Authorised Members → Businesses → Opportunities**

IMBOPwiki adds the knowledge and collaboration layer:

**KNOW → DISCUSS → DISCOVER → ENQUIRE → CONNECT → ACT**

The product should turn useful community knowledge into durable institutional memory without becoming an ungoverned social network.

## 3. What It Is
- Governed knowledge layer.
- Collaborative discussion layer.
- Community and topic layer.
- Structured research, reports and analysis.
- Durable institutional memory.
- Discovery surface connected to businesses and opportunities.
- Communication layer that can work with WhatsApp, email and future channels.

## 4. What It Is Not
- Not a replacement for WhatsApp.
- Not an unrestricted social network.
- Not a generic public wiki.
- Not a document dump.
- Not a conventional website builder.
- Not a standalone chat application.
- Not an automatic publication channel.
- Not a substitute for IMBOP institutional governance.

## 5. Primary Users
- Public visitors who discover published knowledge.
- Authenticated IMBOP members.
- Institution administrators.
- Community contributors.
- Editors/reviewers.
- Moderators.
- SE platform administrators.
- Future specialist roles such as researchers or programme managers.

## 6. Primary Use Cases
### Knowledge
Market trends, sector research, reports and analysis, export/import opportunities, procurement intelligence, value-chain knowledge, how-to/reference material and institutional knowledge.

### Collaboration
Discussions, questions and answers, working groups, collaborative research, opportunity conversations and peer learning.

### Economic Discovery
Knowledge can connect to institutions, members, businesses, products/services, offers, needs and opportunities.

## 7. Product Boundary
IMBOPwiki owns the structured knowledge and governed collaboration record. External channels may distribute or notify about that record.

Example:
**IMBOPwiki article → Notify Community → WhatsApp/email notification → Member returns to IMBOPwiki**

The authoritative content remains inside IMBOPwiki.

## 8. Access Philosophy
**Open to discover; authenticated to participate.**

Publicly published material may be discoverable. Participation requires authentication and the appropriate membership or role. Private institutional and working-group content is additionally protected.

## 9. Governance Philosophy
Default progression:

**DRAFT → SUBMITTED → IN REVIEW → APPROVED → PUBLISHED → REVIEW/UPDATE → ARCHIVED**

Where appropriate:

**RETURN / REJECT / UNPUBLISH**

## 10. MVP Direction
The first release establishes the durable foundation for knowledge records, discussions, communities, topics/tags, governed publication, attribution, version history, institutional/private visibility, search/discovery, links to IMBOP economic objects and in-app notifications.

Rich external integrations, advanced recommendation systems, native apps and sophisticated social mechanics remain outside the first release unless justified.

## 11. Success Definition
IMBOPwiki succeeds when useful knowledge does not disappear into informal conversations.

A useful discussion should be able to become:

**Conversation → Knowledge → Discovery → Economic Action**

The product should strengthen IMBOP's institutional network rather than compete with every communication platform.
