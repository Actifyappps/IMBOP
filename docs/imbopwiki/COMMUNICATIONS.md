# IMBOPwiki — Communications

## 1. Principle
IMBOPwiki separates the authoritative knowledge record from channels used to distribute or discuss it.

**IMBOPwiki owns the record. Channels distribute attention.**

## 2. Internal Channels
Initial channels:
- in-app notifications;
- institutional notices;
- community notifications;
- email.

Future channels:
- WhatsApp;
- SMS;
- push notifications;
- other approved integrations.

## 3. WhatsApp Relationship
WhatsApp complements IMBOPwiki rather than replacing it.

Example:
**New export opportunity brief published → community notification → WhatsApp notification/link → member opens the authoritative IMBOPwiki record → discussion/enquiry continues in IMBOP.**

The authoritative record remains in IMBOPwiki.

## 4. Communication Events
Potential events:
- content submitted;
- content approved;
- content published;
- content returned;
- content rejected;
- discussion reply;
- mention;
- community announcement;
- new opportunity-related content;
- review reminder.

## 5. Delivery
Reuse the existing IMBOP communications architecture where applicable. IMBOPwiki should not create an unrelated email queue/provider architecture.

## 6. Preferences
Users and institutions may eventually control appropriate notification preferences. Preferences must not suppress mandatory governance or security notices.

## 7. Distribution vs Publication
Sending a notification does not publish a record. Publishing does not require every channel to be used.

## 8. Audit
Communication events should retain trigger, audience, channel, delivery state where applicable and provider information where external delivery occurs.
